Model the fiscal core first: legal entities, numbering series, and tax profiles for VAT and IRPF, plus the quote to order/project to invoice chain in Drizzle with strict referential integrity.

Store money as integer minor units, apply per-line tax and Spanish rounding rules, never floats.

Issue fiscal numbers as gapless per-series sequences inside a transaction using row and advisory locks, behind idempotent APIs so retries never duplicate or skip a number.

Make issued documents append-only with a full audit trail and hash-chained records, laying the Verifactu and e-invoicing foundation from day one.

Enforce multi-company isolation with PostgreSQL Row Level Security keyed on org and company.
