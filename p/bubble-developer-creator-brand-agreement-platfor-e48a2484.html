I'd build it in four layers. First, email/password accounts with brand and creator roles, progressive profiles that only ask for details when a step needs them, plus the ToS acceptance flow with a timestamped audit record. Second, Stripe Connect KYC onboarding for both parties. Third, the structured questionnaire feeding your solicitor's JSON clause library into Documint or DocSpring for document assembly, then DocuSign or HelloSign for signature. Fourth, Stripe Connect fund-holding with conditional release on confirmed delivery, dashboards for active and past deals, and automated emails at each step.
