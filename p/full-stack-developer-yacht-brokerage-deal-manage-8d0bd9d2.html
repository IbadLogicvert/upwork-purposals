Phase 2 first - port the prototype to Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind, wire the Supabase JS client for auth and session, and connect the deal forms, document library, pricing waterfall, and checklist to your 13 tables so RLS keeps each broker isolated. Wire the admin panel to Supabase Auth for invite/edit/deactivate, then deploy to Vercel on your domain.

Phase 3 - the PDF engine. Your field maps make this fast: pdf-lib fills the AcroForm fields for the YBAA PSA (all 68) and Acceptance of Vessel; ReportLab handles coordinate overlay for the 5 flat PDFs (CG-1340, MD DNR B-240, Warranty of Title, Loan Payoff, POAs), saved to the deal-docs bucket.

Phase 4 - Dropbox Sign: send from the deal document view, webhook to flip document status, signed PDF back to Supabase.

Phase 5 - mobile responsive, per-deal activity log, Resend notifications on signed and stage change, custom SMTP for invites.
