For real iOS and Android builds I would lead with FlutterFlow over Bubble, since it compiles to native apps you can push to the App Store and Play Store. Steps:

1. Model the data - clients, taskers, task categories, bookings, reviews - in Firebase or Supabase.

2. Build role-based signup and onboarding so clients and taskers get separate flows.

3. Task posting, browsing by category, and the match and accept booking loop.

4. In-app chat plus push notifications for new tasks and messages.

5. Stripe Connect for payments with a hold-and-release on job completion.

6. Ratings and reviews, then build and submit to TestFlight and Play Console.
